The Harness Is Part of the Benchmark

A controlled audit of prompt and extraction sensitivity in POPE.

Project overview

A measurement audit for multimodal benchmarks

Research question

How much of a reported POPE score comes from the model, and how much comes from the prompt and extraction harness around it?

Experimental control

Qwen2.5-VL-7B outputs are frozen once, then replayed under multiple prompt and answer-parsing configurations.

Dataset scale

36,000 JSONL responses were generated for the controlled audit. Only compact aggregate results are tracked in Git.

The central finding

TL;DR

!

By holding Qwen2.5-VL-7B's outputs completely fixed, we demonstrate that benchmark scores are largely artifacts of the evaluation harness. A strict parser fails completely (F1 = 0.000), while a loose parser recovers high capability (F1 = 0.836).

Scope

Limitations

Due to shared-server network restrictions blocking AWS and PyPI, this audit evaluated only one model: Qwen2.5-VL-7B. Even within this single-model study, the effect is so extreme that it is highly likely to generalize to other model-and-harness combinations.
